The Loop

How It Works

From first login to restored kingdom in four beats.

Create your Adventurer

Pick an avatar, claim a name, and awaken in the Tutorial Village where the Old Sage awaits.

Explore a Realm

Roam with WASD, talk to NPCs, accept quests, loot chests and dodge roaming Glitchlings.

Solve the Runestones

Each runestone opens a real coding trial. Pass hidden tests to restore the seal and weaken the boss.

Slay, Level, Ascend

Defeat the realm boss in a code-battle, earn loot and XP, and unlock the next realm of the kingdom.
